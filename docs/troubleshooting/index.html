Troubleshooting

The errors you might hit in the labs, with their likely causes and fixes. Errors from setting up and deploying, such as a missing extension or an unavailable region, are under Setup and deployment, and the others are under their lab, with the steps where they show up. Look for the error code or the message, for example 429 or ValidationError. On the site, the Troubleshoot button of a lab step shows only that step's errors.
